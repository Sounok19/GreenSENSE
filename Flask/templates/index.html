Spot problems before they spread

Plant diseases leave patterns on leaves, but reading them takes time and expertise that most growers cannot spare. GreenSENSE uses image analysis and a deep-learning classifier to recognize those patterns and give you a head start.

GreenSENSE is a decision aid, not a lab test. For severe or unclear cases, check with a local agronomist.
